Copom Statement - 174th Meeting
Banco Central do Brasil, 17 April 2013

The Copom released the following note to the public:

"The Copom decided to increase the Selic rate to 7.50 percent, without bias, with 6 votes for the monetary policy action and 2 votes in favor of maintaining the Selic rate at 7.25 percent.

The Committee evaluates that the high inflation level and the dispersion in price increases, among other factors, contribute for inflation to show resilience and demand a monetary policy response. On the other hand, the Copom evaluates that domestic and, mainly, external uncertainties surround the prospective scenario for inflation and recommend that monetary policy be administered with caution.

The following members of the Committee voted for the increase of the Selic rate to 7.50 percent p.a.: Alexandre Antonio Tombini (Governor), Altamir Lopes, Anthero de Moraes Meirelles, Carlos Hamilton Vasconcelos Araújo, Luiz Edson Feltrim and Sidnei Corrêa Marques.

The following members of the Committee voted for the maintenance of the Selic rate at 7.25 percent p.a.: Aldo Luiz Mendes and Luiz Awazu Pereira da Silva."

The April Copom Minutes will be released in Portuguese next Thursday, April 25.
