In their discussion of monetary policy for this meeting, participants generally judged that
it was appropriate to maintain the current restrictive stance of monetary policy. Participants
noted that while inflation had declined over the past year, it remained above the Committee's
2 percent longer-run goal. Some participants mentioned that they would be willing to tighten
policy further if inflation were to prove more persistent than expected.

Participants broadly supported keeping the target range for the federal funds rate unchanged.
Several participants noted that, given the data on inflation received so far this year, they
had moved their assessment of the timing of rate reduction later than earlier anticipated.

With respect to the outlook for monetary policy, participants reaffirmed that they did not
expect it would be appropriate to reduce the target range until they had gained greater
confidence that inflation was moving sustainably toward 2 percent.
